## Composition notes

- Burger: landscape closeup with dark background, ideal card cover. This is a thick burger with bacon and plantain chips, not a thin smash burger.
- Chicken and rice: square overhead, centered plate. Center crop works.
- Poke: portrait overhead, centered bowl slightly below image midpoint. Use object-position: 50% 55% for wide cards.
- Pizza: portrait overhead with vivid pink background. Center crop works.
- Fried chicken: portrait overhead, warm wood and golden chicken. Center crop works.
- Shrimp: landscape, wooden table, blue plate. Center crop works.
